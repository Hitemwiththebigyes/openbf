# MINE / issue #43 — local implementation

Committed on `main` on top of `75be240`, with the checkout's existing uncommitted dispenser/grenade work preserved.

The explosive slots now build through the dispenser/detonator and mine class chains. The runtime places items using authored strength, velocity, release delay and `MaxItems`; simulates gravity, rebound and friction; arms at landing; and selects the authored trigger, expiry or destroyed explosion. BF1 mines pass through soldiers and vehicles. BF2 items stick to the world and vehicle hulls, follow hull translation/rotation, and bounce off soldiers. Mine proximity uses closest approach, contact triggering and the optional friendly suppression radius. BF2 `ImmuneToMines` is respected. A detonator consumes a fresh press while a pack exists, including with empty ammunition, without converting the held button or its release into another throw. Bots do not select these weapons for attacks.

Placed items and remote-input history participate in the deterministic sim hash. Bolts and blasts damage the placed items; destroyed packs use the harmless destroyed class. Models, throw animation, explosion effects and explosion/contact/timer sounds are connected. The hand renderer uses the class's held geometry, its offhand geometry, or its placed-item geometry when the class supplies no held model.

## Coverage

The data integration test checks the unchanged disc census: **BF1 10 classes / 5 explosive weapon kinds; BF2 43 classes / 12 kinds**. Every carrying class retains the right slot and ammunition, resolves all named explosion classes, loads its held/placed models, places and arms an item, then triggers it through proximity, its timer or the next press. Nonremote dispensers also exercise their authored limit and oldest-item replacement. That limit check holds item lifetime at infinity after placement to isolate the limit from time-bomb expiry; separate tests exercise the real timers.

Focused simulation tests cover landing, same-owner limits versus a different owner, closest approach at 1.2 m, friends, standing enemies, the BF1 four-metre lethal blast, 10 s / 5 s expiry while airborne, remote detonation with empty ammunition, a held remote press with ammunition still available, harmless destruction by a shot, friendly suppression, engineer immunity, contact triggering, hull attachment and replay state.

The class-kit expectation changes exactly the 53 formerly omitted explosive slots to working dispensers, preserving the authored slot/channel/ammunition entries.

## Assets and remaining approximations

`scripts/rebake.sh` now includes the common landmine, placed landmine, seismic charge and BF2 detpack models in the side weapon bake, plus BF1 REP's hand mine. Targeted `bake weapon` runs refreshed all eight affected game/model combinations and their textures/nodes. Copyrighted assets remain local and ignored.

The new item collision uses a provisional 0.15 m sphere for bullet hits/blast falloff and the existing soldier/vehicle bounds for proximity. The terrain contact is snapped to the heightfield surface after the existing approximate terrain ray; bounce clearance is capped at 1 cm so the displacement cannot keep an item bouncing above the landing-speed threshold. BF1 release uses the existing soldier fire-point approximation; BF2 uses the existing cached offhand-offset approximation. Timer sounds currently use the sound bank's pitch; the original rising tick pitch is not reproduced. These are not claimed as measured PS2 trajectory/audio parity.
