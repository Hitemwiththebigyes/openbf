# ATUR / issue #45 — local implementation

Working tree on `main` on top of `48df138`, not committed.

Every BF2 sniper class now builds its auto-turret slot; before, the slot was left out. A press of the dispenser throws the class's `portableturret` entity the way the original's dispenser does. The throw speed is the charge-scaled `MinStrength`..`MaxStrength` × `Velocity` (3–4 m/s) plus the owner's velocity, and the turret takes the owner's team. It falls by the original's half-step gravity. On a contact whose normal has y > 0.7 it lands upright (frame up = the normal). On anything steeper it bounces by the class's `Friction` and `Rebound`, using the mine's bounce formula, now shared. A second throw past `MaxItems` 1 kills the first turret and replaces it.

Once it has landed, the turret's own stationary controller thinks every 0.5 s:
- It looks for the nearest visible enemy within 100 m that the gun's `AITarget` mask allows.
- It fights a target inside the gun's MinRange..MaxRange (0..40 m) and keeps the fight out to 50 m.
- It fires only inside 40 m with the aim within 22.5°.
- With no target it sweeps an idle look of ±30° about its landing heading, redrawn every 18 thinks.

It turns by the hover stick law `u|u|` at `MaxTurnSpeed` / `MaxPitchSpeed` 3.8 within `PitchLimits` ±35°. The cannon fires its own salvos (4 bolts, 0.15–0.3 s apart, every 1.7 s; 100 m/s, 100 damage), and kills credit the turret's owner.

The turret has 50 health. Bolts and blasts hurt it by their Building scale, so a BF2 rifle bolt does a tenth. It explodes with its class's `ExplosionName` (50 damage over 4 m) when it is destroyed, when it expires at its 60 s `Lifetime`, and when a new throw replaces it. Falling below the world floor removes it quietly.

A bot throws one by the original's rule (`FUN_002b8400`). Its target must be a soldier on foot in a cluster of his own side: more than 3 within 9 m and none hostile, the grenade's group test. The dispenser must also be ready. There is no range test and no test for a turret that still stands. The rate is set by the one round per life, the 2 s reload and `MaxItems`. The bot's weapon choice never takes the dispenser.

The game draws the `com_weap_inf_dropturret` model with its own sheet, `com_prop_dropturret_rep`, which is only in the side LVLs. The model hangs on its own geometry about 0.8 m above the landing point, turned so its front (+Z, the gun) faces the aim, and blended between ticks (card RINT) like the droid. The band's UVs scroll at the disc's rate in the world and on the HUD's item icon. It plays the explosion's effect and sound, and the headless event log records the turret's end (`turret_gone`) and bolt hits on it (`t<id>`). Turrets, their gun state and their targets are in the deterministic sim hash (`TURRETS`).

Shared code instead of copies:
- `Sim::throw_from`: mines, droids, packs and turrets.
- `oldest_beyond`: mines and turrets.
- `mine::fall` / `mine::rebound` and `Sim::sweep_item`: mines and turrets.
- `Sim::free_shots`: the droid's blaster and the turret's gun.
- `remote::look_frame` / `tilted`: droids and turrets.
- `Sim::item_damage` / `damage_item`: mines, droids and turrets, from bolts and blasts. The bolt arms in `combat.rs` and the blast passes in `blast.rs` are now one each.
- `Sim::vehicle_sphere`.
- `render_blend::blend_items`: droids and turrets.

## Playtest revision

The user's playtest against the original found four faults. The fixes:

| Reported | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The HUD icon was a white circle | `com_prop_dropturret_rep` was never baked: the model is in INGAME.LVL, its sheet only in the SIDE LVLs | `bake weapon` takes several LVLs and poses a bone-space model; `scripts/rebake.sh` bakes the turret from INGAME + SIDE/REP into `side_bf2` |
| The turret sat on the ground | the port put the model's sphere bottom on the ground | the origin is the landing point, so the sphere hangs at `boneroot`, 1.056 m up |
| Flat, unanimated look | no sheet (white), and the band's BF2 UV scroll (MTRL flag 0x1000000, bytes 200 / 255) was not read | the sheet is baked; `Segment::uv_scroll` → GLB `uv_scroll` → a scrolling texture in the world and a scrolled part on a repeating HUD sampler |
| Bots spammed turrets | our rule threw whenever an enemy was within 40 m and no turret of theirs lived; the turret dispenser was also missing from `dispenses()`, so the bot's weapon choice could take it | the original's cluster rule; `dispenses()` includes it |

A second playtest found the turret drawn as not firing: its bolts, muzzle flash and fire animation appeared at the owning sniper's gun. The sim fired from the turret's muzzle, but `Event::Shot` names the owner (for credit and sound), and the game drew every on-foot owner's shot from that soldier's fire point. `Sim::is_item_gun` now marks a droid's or turret's gun class; `bolt_vis` draws those bolts from their own spawn, and `soldier_vis` neither flashes nor animates the owner for them. No muzzle flash is drawn at the turret either: only a soldier's held gun draws one, and no turret, vehicle or building code calls the weapon render (`re-muzzle-flash.md` §5), so the gun's authored `med_muzzle_flash` is never shown, as the third playtest confirmed. The droid's blaster had the same fault. Tests: `a_turrets_bolt_is_drawn_from_its_own_gun_not_its_owners_hand` (fails without the fix), `its_bolts_leave_its_own_gun_far_from_its_owner`, `an_item_guns_shot_never_flashes_or_animates_its_owner`, and the real-disc suite's shot-origin check.

Also fixed: the model was drawn back to front (its gun is at +Z). The muzzle now sits at `aimer_x` + `hp_fire`, and the hit sphere is the shell's 0.25 m.

## Coverage

The data integration test `game/tests/suite/auto_turrets.rs` covers all **14 BF2 classes** that carry the turret: the four `<side>_inf_default_sniper` classes and their variants. For every one it:
- builds the slot (slot 4, channel 1, 1 clip);
- checks the dispenser (`MaxItems` 1, strength 0.3–0.4, `Velocity` 10, `ReloadTime` 2);
- checks the turret (health 50, lifetime 60, Building, turn rates 3.8, pitch ±35°, full-circle yaw, model, 50-damage explosion);
- checks the gun (MaxRange 40, `ShotDelay` 1.7, salvo of 4, bolt 100 m/s / 0.8 s / 100 damage);
- loads the model from `common_bf2/models` and `side_bf2/weapons`, finds the band's scroll in both, and finds the baked sheet;
- deploys the turret, sees its origin land on the ground, fires at least one salvo at an enemy 20 m out, and sees it expire at 60 s ± 0.2.

Focused tests:
- 4 in `core/src/autoturret/tests.rs`: class keys and constructor defaults, lifetime and destroyed ends, landing and the steep-wall bounce, the turn law and pitch limits.
- 8 in `core/src/sim/turrets/tests.rs`:
  - deploy lands it upright, its origin on the ground;
  - an enemy at 20 m is shot dead for the owner;
  - an enemy at 60 m is tracked with no shot, then fired on once it closes;
  - a building-immune blast does nothing, a 0.1-scaled bolt takes 10, and a blast destroys it;
  - the replacement throw and the 60 s expiry;
  - a bot throws only at a soldier in a cluster of his side: not at a lone one, not with a friend of the bot within 9 m of him;
  - over a long fight (150 s, 3 rounds, an unkillable cluster of 5) a bot throws 3 times, at least the 2 s reload apart, never has more than 1 alive, and 2 are replaced; with a lone enemy it throws none in 60 s;
  - replay hashing.
- 1 in `mungefs/src/model.rs`: the band's MTRL (0x01010010, bytes 200 / 255) scrolls at (−200/255, −1); without 0x1000000, or in BF1, it does not.
- 1 in `ui/src/hud_draw.rs`: a scrolling HUD mesh part's UVs move with the HUD clock.
- 1 in `game/src/render_blend.rs`: a turret drawn between its ticks.

The class-kit expectation changes exactly the 14 formerly omitted slots to `auto-turret x1`.

## Remaining approximations

- [H] Expiry and replacement explode through `ExplosionName`. The original kills through the damage interface, and the explosion on that path was not traced to the call.
- [H] The turret outlives its owner and cannot be repaired.
- [H] The update's third kill (flag +0x228 bit 3 clear with class +0x384 and +0x4d4 zero) is not modelled.
- OURS:
  - the 0.5 s think;
  - holding the trigger while the gun's `ShotDelay` and salvo pace the shots;
  - the aim as a world-frame yaw / pitch.
- [D] The landing puts the entity's origin on the contact point; its collision primitive was not read. The reference screenshot fits.
- [H] Whether the cluster count holds the target (the grenade's `is_group`, shared).
- The model is drawn rigid, turned by yaw, with no pitch pose. The band is drawn with the character light, not unlit. `OverrideTexture` is not a general mechanism; for this model every played side names the sheet the model already uses.
- The scroll restarts with the renderer's 2000 s clock wrap, a one-frame jump every 33 minutes (the water's rates are whole repeats at 2000 s, the band's are not).
- `TurretAmbientSound`, `TurretActivateSound` and the per-contact collision sounds are not played.
- Soldiers walk through the turret, and enemy bots do not pick it as a target; they hit it only incidentally.

## Validation

Final runs, after the third playtest (no turret muzzle flash):
- `cargo fmt --all -- --check`: clean.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- `cargo test --workspace --lib --bins --tests --example arena --no-fail-fast`: **2474 passed, 1 failed, 55 ignored**. That includes the new focused tests and the real-data integration test.

The one failure is `bake::shell::tests::the_baked_shell_holds_the_menu_loops_and_the_music`: the local shell bake has no `iface_instant_action` sheet. The bake predates `0a3d190`, which added that sheet; this change touches neither the test nor the shell bake. The CPU-time test `nav_walls::mygeetos_position_table_is_built_in_under_a_third_of_a_second` passed in this run. The ignored tests keep their existing GPU, manual and environment requirements.

`cargo build --release -p game` built the release game, and its `--write-pack` rebuilt `dist/openbf.exe`: it was staged as `dist/openbf.exe.new`, then swapped in, and `dist/openbf_data` was kept. The exported checkout has no `engine/artifacts/loop/STATUS.md`, no autoloop gate baselines and no stored PCSX2 reference captures. So no sweep or arena baseline equality is claimed, and no live original-game comparison. This local work closes no upstream issue or PR.

To try it: pick a BF2 sniper, for example `rep_inf_default_sniper`, on any BF2 map. Switch to the secondary weapon (slot 4) and press secondary fire.
